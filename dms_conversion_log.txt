  * GETDATE() → NOW() (current timestamp)
  * DATEDIFF → AGE function with DATE_PART to extract years
- DATEPART(YEAR, HireDate) → DATE_PART('year', HireDate)
  * DATEPART → DATE_PART with lowercase 'year'
